## Audience strategies


![](https://docs.tealium.com/images/icons/icon-cart-shopping.svg)

#### Cart abandonment

Target visitors who added products to their cart but didn't complete a purchase.



![](https://docs.tealium.com/images/icons/icon-shop.svg)

#### Browse abandonment

Target visitors who browsed products without adding them to their cart.



![](https://docs.tealium.com/images/icons/icon-users-viewfinder.svg)

#### Abandonment strategy

Understand abandonment types and build retargeting campaigns.



![](https://docs.tealium.com/images/icons/icon-cart-spending.svg)

#### Customer spending levels

Target visitors based on how much they have spent within a time frame.



![](https://docs.tealium.com/images/icons/icon-percent-badge.svg)

#### Discount shopper

Target visitors who browse discounted products.



![](https://docs.tealium.com/images/icons/icon-users-slash.svg)

#### Exclude offline purchasers

Remove offline purchasers from online remarketing campaigns.



![](https://docs.tealium.com/images/icons/icon-crown.svg)

#### VIP badge

Identify and target your highest-value returning customers.



![](https://docs.tealium.com/images/icons/icon-user-check.svg)

#### Known visitor badge

Create a badge to identify visitors who have provided a unique identifier.



![](https://docs.tealium.com/images/icons/icon-money-chart.svg)

#### Lifetime value

Track total customer revenue and build strategies around customer value.
